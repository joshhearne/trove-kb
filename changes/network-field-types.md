type: feature
audience: everyone

- New field types for network details: **IP address** (with a prefix), **Network (CIDR)**, **IP range**, **MAC address**, **VLAN ID** and **Hostname**. A network shows its mask, broadcast and usable range for you.
- **Port**, **Port range** and **AS number** fields read what you paste, such as `443/tcp`, `https` or `AS13335`.
- **IP address list** and **Port list** fields hold several entries in order, such as DNS servers or the ports a firewall rule opens.
