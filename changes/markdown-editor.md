Markdown editors for documents and knowledge base articles now have:
- a toolbar, with **indent** and **outdent** buttons (Tab and Shift+Tab inside a list).
- a **Preview** tab that shows the text exactly as the saved page will.
- on a wide screen, the preview **beside** the text as you type, with a handle to share the width.
Choose how editors open, **Visual**, **Markdown**, or **Markdown with live preview**, from your account menu.
