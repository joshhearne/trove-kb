- Knowledge base articles now show **Linked from**: the articles and documents that link to them. Documents also list the articles that link to them.
- **Runbooks inside runbooks**: a step that is only a `[[link]]` to another runbook opens into that runbook's steps, so one onboarding runbook can list the small runbooks it needs. Tick the steps one by one, or tick the linked step to tick all of its steps at once.
